# Evidence: /ollama forwards proxy credentials and browser headers upstream

Request to the local proxy:
  GET /ollama/api/version
  Authorization: Bearer <TEST_TOKEN>
  X-Proxy-Token: <TEST_TOKEN>
  Cookie: session=leak-me
  Origin: http://127.0.0.1:45873

Headers observed by the audit upstream (127.0.0.1:45872):
{
  "host": "127.0.0.1:45872",
  "authorization": "Bearer <TEST_TOKEN>",
  "x-proxy-token": "<TEST_TOKEN>",
  "cookie": "session=leak-me",
  "origin": "http://127.0.0.1:45873"
}

Cause: server.ts:233-236 copies every inbound header except HOP_BY_HOP.
Neither authorization nor cookie nor x-proxy-token is stripped before the
upstream fetch. If OLLAMA_HOST points at a remote or untrusted service, the
shared secret is disclosed to it and may be captured in its logs.
